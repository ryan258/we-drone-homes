It made sense on the whiteboard.

Our founder had a camera drone, a friend with a real estate license, and the kind of confidence that should require a second signature.

“We drone homes,” he said. It was concise. It was descriptive. The domain was available. Nobody in the room worked in public relations.

Two hundred business cards, forty-eight embroidered polos, and one very memorable vehicle wrap later, a homeowner asked us to leave. We hadn’t even unloaded the tripod.
