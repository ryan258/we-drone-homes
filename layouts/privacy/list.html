This is a parody.

We Drone Homes is a fictional real estate drone photography outfit. All crew biographies, testimonials, property listings, equipment claims, performance numbers, prices, and incidents are invented. Illustrative images are not evidence of real shoots. There are no services to purchase here.

The forms stay in your browser.

The mock booking tool builds a fictional mission brief from the options you select. It does not ask for your name, email, phone number, or real property address. It sends no booking request, reserves no appointment, and takes no payment. The mock waiver is not an agreement and has no legal effect.

Package links can include the package identifier in the URL. The site does not use cookies, analytics scripts, or browser storage. Form selections remain in the current page; your browser may independently restore form fields when you return. There is no cross-device account or saved booking.

Downloads are your choice.

The “Save mission brief” button creates a text file locally. Nothing is uploaded. Any downloaded copy remains on your device until you delete it.

Hosting still works like hosting.

Your browser requests pages and images from the server hosting this site. A hosting provider may log those requests, including network information. There are no third-party fonts, embedded media, or social widgets built into these pages.

Sound and motion are optional.

Soundboard audio starts only when you activate a sound pad. The flight-path simulation starts only when you press Play. Reduced-motion preferences are respected, and the simulation also has a manual progress control.
