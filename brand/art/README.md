## Final generation prompts

### architectural

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: lead landscape campaign photograph for the We Drone Homes fictional agency brand, text and logos overlaid separately. A modest mid-century suburban ranch with cedar, pale limestone and soft moss green lawn, on a slightly sloping manicured lot; a barely visible tiny camera quad above the lawn. Editorial architectural photography from a low aerial three-quarter angle at 35mm equivalent, immaculate but recognizably ordinary property, not a luxury compound. Warm late-afternoon light, evergreen shadows, pale stone, faint butter yellow highlights, realistic restraint, slight fine film grain. Place house center-right with the left third trees and garden; preserve composition for a center square crop. Wide landscape 3:2. No writing, UI, marks, people, weapons, damage, signage, or logos.

### chandelier

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: portrait campaign photograph for a satirical fictional real estate agency. Still from an impossible but meticulously composed fictional indoor low-altitude flythrough in an upscale, human-scale suburban dining room; viewed from directly below a large, pristine crystal chandelier centered slightly right of frame above an oak table, softly lit glazing, pale plaster, evergreen accents. Visual tension from a very small unobtrusive dark cinewhoop seen farther into the room. No collision. Crisp glass details, cream and dark green shadows, warm evening color; sophisticated property-magazine photograph, gentle grain. Portrait 2:3, good central crop for a social panel. No writing, icons, HUD, people, damage, military references or logos.

### flight-desk

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: landscape brand texture photograph for a fictional aviation-themed property marketing studio. Overhead still life on an off-white architect's worktable: folded graphite line residential floor plan without labels, two lengths of fine surveyor's string, a single yellow grease pencil, a plain olive green folder, edge of a tiny black drone rotor and matte black camera partly visible. Architectural practice meets suburban flight planning, deliberate asymmetry, daylight raking across dry paper grain. Low saturation, ink evergreen and warm paper, concentrated acid chartreuse pencil accent; leave a calm area upper right. 3:2 landscape. No legible writing, pseudo-text, insignia, people, logos, weapons, damage or digital HUD.
